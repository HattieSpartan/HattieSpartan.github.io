About

From actuarial maths to the support floor, and back to the data.

I’m a business and data analyst in Nairobi. My path hasn’t been a straight line, but each step has fed the next: a quantitative foundation, then real operational work, then the technical depth to do more with what I saw there.

  1. Graduated 2022

    BSc Actuarial Science, South Eastern Kenya University

    Probability, statistics, risk and financial mathematics. I also served as treasurer of the student actuarial association, managing its budget and financial reporting. The degree gave me the habit of treating numbers as claims that need evidence.

  2. 2023

    Data science training and a first internship

    I completed a Certificate in Data Science at Moringa School and a remote data analytics internship at MeriSkill: cleaning data, exploratory analysis and early predictive models in Python and SQL. I had the tools, but not yet the real problems.

  3. May – Sep 2024

    Customer Service Representative, CCI Kenya

    Handling up to 60Handling 60–70 customer emails a dayndash;70 customer emails a day for an e-commerce marketplace, while meeting QA and customer-satisfaction targets. Working the queue myself is where I learned how processes actually break for the people inside them. I was promoted within three months.

  4. Sep 2024 – Dec 2025

    Quality Analyst, CCI Kenya

    Auditing 50–60 customer interactions a day as one of six QAs covering 23–25 agents, and taking part in weekly calibration with the client and our team leads. I took on work beyond the role: owning the analysis of customer dissatisfaction scores, and building the reports our QA lead used and I presented to the campaign’s senior manager. This is where analysis stopped being an exercise and started changing what people did.

  5. Sep 2025 – 2027 (expected)

    MSc Data Science, KCA University

    Machine learning, NLP, computer vision and data engineering, and a research project on claims reserving that brings my actuarial background back into the picture.

  6. Now

    Business and data analytics

    I’m looking for a data analyst, business analyst or data quality role where I can do what I’m best at: understand the operation, investigate the data honestly, and turn it into a decision someone can act on.

What ties it together

Actuarial science taught me to be rigorous. Customer support taught me that behind every KPI is a person following a process that may or may not make sense. Quality analytics taught me to connect the two. Data science is how I scale that up.

I’m not trying to be the person who knows every tool. I want to be the person who can walk into an unfamiliar problem, work out what’s really going on, and explain it clearly enough that something changes.
