How I work

Analysis is only useful if it changes what someone does next.

01

Start from the operation

I’ve answered the customer emails, audited them, and coached from the results. I know what a KPI looks like from the inside, which helps me ask better questions of the data.

02

Check the numbers, including my own

When a result contradicts something I trust, I go back to the definition. That habit caught a counting error in my QA analysis and a bias in my own spam classifier.

03

End with something usable

A coaching priority, a clearer SOP, a cheat sheet, or an app someone can type into. I write up what I found so that people who weren’t in the room can act on it.
